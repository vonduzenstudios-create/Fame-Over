# Starter brief: the chef (pilot character 2)

Proposed answers to the art prompt's interview. Everything here is a proposal for Matthew to confirm or
change; lines marked **Open** need his answer. Gameplay needs from the specials: a high-arc projectile,
a low projectile that travels along the floor, and a low strike with the prop. All are kitchen gear.

**A. Identity**
1. id `chef`; display name **Open**.
2. A furious celebrity head chef who treats every fight like a failing kitchen service: shouting,
   pointing, impatient, explosive.
3. Visual style of the earlier British chef placeholder: spiky blond hair, white chef's jacket, red
   neckerchief. **Open:** an original chef with those broad traits (default), or a recognisable likeness
   of a real chef (only with a decision on permission).

**B. Look**
4. Lean and athletic, mid-50s; upright standing height 500 px.
5. Short spiky sandy-blond hair, deep forehead lines, strong jaw, clean-shaven, fair skin.
6. White double-breasted chef's jacket with sleeves rolled to the forearm, red neckerchief, charcoal
   trousers, black work shoes. Jacket, neckerchief, trousers and shoes identical on every frame.
7. Neckerchief ends may move.

**C. Fighting style**
8. Orthodox, left foot forward, guard high and tight, weight forward (aggressive).
9. Brisk and twitchy: quick steps, constantly leaning in.
10. Brawler: hooks and jabs thrown hard, scrappy stomping kicks.
11. Throw: grabs the jacket collar and shoves the opponent away with a headbutt.

**D. Signature prop**
12. A wooden rolling pin, about 45 cm, light wood with darker handles.
13. Stowed through the back of the belt on the right hip, the same on every frame.
14. Used in: `hk` (replaced by an overhead rolling-pin chop) and the Rolling Pin Trip special.
    Everything else is unarmed.

**E. Specials (2)**
1. **Onion Bomb** (projectile, two versions): pulls a whole raw onion from his apron pocket and throws
   it like a grenade. It bursts on contact in a stinging cloud of onion fumes and flying layers. High lob
   (`sp-lob-high`) arcs far, low toss (`sp-lob-low`) bowls it rolling along the floor. Body 6-7 frames each
   (reach to pocket, wind up, throw, follow-through). FX `fx-onion` (onion spinning or rolling, 4-frame
   loop, about 70x70 px) and `fx-onion-burst` (fume cloud with onion layers, 5 frames, about 200x200 px).
   The onion only appears in the hand from the reach frame onward; the pocket looks the same on every frame.
2. **Rolling Pin Trip** (low strike): pulls the rolling pin and sweeps it low in front, tripping the
   opponent. Body 7-8 frames (draw pin, low swing, re-stow). No FX.

**F. Presentation**
19. Intro: slams a hand flat on an invisible pass, points at the opponent, raises his guard.
20. Victory: throws his arms wide and shouts, then a dismissive wave.
21. Dizzy: staggers with a hand on his forehead, muttering.
22. Portrait: mid-shout, eyebrows down.
23. Idle: impatient bounce on the front foot, jaw working.
