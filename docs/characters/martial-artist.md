# Starter brief: the martial artist (pilot character 1)

Proposed answers to the art prompt's interview. Everything here is a proposal for Matthew to confirm or
change; lines marked **Open** need his answer.

**A. Identity**
1. id `martialartist` for now; display name **Open**.
2. A calm, unbeatable action-movie martial artist. Quiet confidence, economical movement, never wastes
   a motion; a hint of deadpan humour.
3. Look inspired by the broad traits of a 1980s action-movie martial artist (rugged, bearded, red-haired).
   **Open:** keep it an original character with only those broad traits (default), or a recognisable
   likeness of a real actor (only with a decision on permission).

**B. Look**
4. Stocky, powerful, about 50; standing height about 510 px.
5. Short reddish-brown hair, full trimmed beard, weathered tan skin, steady squint.
6. Denim shirt with sleeves rolled to the elbow, open at the collar; dark blue jeans; brown leather belt
   with a plain buckle; brown cowboy-style boots. Shirt, jeans and boots identical on every frame.
7. No loose accessories; the shirt tail may move slightly.

**C. Fighting style**
8. Orthodox, left foot and fist forward, guard at chin height, weight centred.
9. Planted and deliberate: small precise steps, no bounce.
10. Martial-arts kicks are the star: fast chambered kicks, short straight punches.
11. Throw: catches the arm and flips the opponent over the hip.

**D. Signature prop**
12. None. Fights barehanded.

**E. Specials (2)**
1. **Spirit Bolt** (projectile): a sharp two-handed push from the hip that fires a glowing bolt of
   energy straight ahead. Body 6-8 frames (gather, push, recover). FX `fx-bolt`: a travelling bolt
   about 120x60 px, 4-frame loop, plus a 3-frame burst on hit. **Open:** bolt colour (default white-gold).
2. **Flying Kick** (rush): leaps forward feet-first across about half the screen, a straight flying side
   kick. Body 6-8 frames (crouch, launch, extended kick held in flight, landing). FX `fx-dust` on take-off.

**F. Presentation**
19. Intro: walks in, cracks his neck, settles into the stance.
20. Victory: turns side-on, arms folded, a single nod.
21. Dizzy: sways with eyes half shut, shakes his head.
22. Portrait: stern half-smile.
23. Idle: slow steady breathing, an occasional small shift of the lead hand.
